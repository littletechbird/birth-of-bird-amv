## Diminishing returns

- Variants past 2–3 rarely fix continuity.
- Don’t primary Seedance on every pocket — reserve heroes for peaks.
- $150→$300 unlocks stubborn heroes; $300→$500 = comfort + retries, not a new physics.
- **G_plan = ceil(1.5 × (N − 1))** unique motion headroom costs more gens up front and saves reverse/pad tax later.
