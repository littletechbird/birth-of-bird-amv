# Birth of Bird — Craft Laws

**Status:** HARD public checklist. Continuity > novelty. Chip-away > flood.

If a choice fights this sheet, stop and re-read.

---

## Quality bar (ceiling, not floor)

- **Visuals:** aim Runaway-class — cinematic motion, readable silhouettes, no meh mid-clip mush.
- **Music / 808s:** aim Epic of BLUE weight — serious silk, not comedy.
- Title frame: **The Birth of Bird**; on-screen **BIRD** in **red** (Akira / Runaway red energy).
- Nobody dies. Chooses good. Ocean calms. Friends return.

---

## Half-split / motion headroom

- Total picture scales to the locked song length (**D**).
- Mileposts: **N** still anchors (classic skeleton used N=22 for D≈240s; scale with D).
- Pocket length: **Δ ≈ D / (N − 1)** between consecutive anchors.
- Default i2v duration request: ~11–12 seconds when the tool allows.
- Chapter air OK: hard cut and/or silence / story-only beat between B–I–R–D.
- **HARD unique-motion headroom:** plan **`G_plan = ceil(1.5 × (N − 1))`** unique real-motion gens. Never assemble short and pad with stills / Ken Burns / recycles / reverse / ping-pong.

---

## Body / wing / mouth laws

- **No tail.** Human body + **bird head**.
- Wings **hidden under jacket** until **The Reveal**.
- Fly = jacket off → breast/back feathers + wings out (Archangel scale for Littlebird).
- **Beaks CLOSED always** — even in talk scenes. Eyes / body / wings carry emotion. No lipsync fight.
- **No mammal ears** on any bird character.
- **No dancing** from main cast — grounded, breath, fight, flight/reveal. Drama tone, not comedy dance AMV.

---

## Cast / POV

- **POV:** Littlebird / Little Tech Bird eyes only.
- Birdneda = eagle (friend / lead / search — not a dark villain).
- Buzz Boys keep the runt (pressure; mix of light flock + dark street energy).
- Falcon = she, kind eyes / shy; soft-color jacket (never black/charcoal).
- Littlebird = boy runt, **smallest** in frame; orange bomber intact until Reveal; Reveal wings = **blue/cyan** like his head.
- Dark fight = one taste of resentment (The Fall graft: one taste too much / festers grows fills). Foul = spirited OK; **never anger**.

---

## World / art direction

- Art lane: clean modern **anime** · crisp line · cel-shade · punchy contrast — **adult**, not cartoon.
- World = **Neo-Tokyo** (Akira density: cars, buildings, night streets) — **Bird’s city**, not a shot-for-shot rip.
- **Never barn. Never nest. Never happy farm.**
- Backgrounds = IRL-lifelike density; characters = cinematic anime.
- BLUE character zoom: medium-with-air so the scene breathes; establishing shots where the city is a **third character**.
- Vary zoom + blocking across the cut.

---

## Gen discipline (Anti-Flywheel)

- **Chip-away only** — log gens per keeper.
- Cap ~2–3 variants per intended anchor unless the desk opens the gate.
- Jitter-before-order: explore a little variance, then lock order / pick keepers — do not reorder the story to chase a pretty accident.
- Mute native i2v audio; track owns energy.
- Kill bad continuity early — salvage cost often exceeds a few new gens.
- Free-first when free buckets exist; free does not skip Real LOOK QA.

## Lyric-anchor desk + finish

- Lyric-waypoint stickies locked to timecodes; keepers in story order.
- Gap fill = variable-length **city establishers** (character-free) after bricks are stuck.
- Late finish: no new character images that break exact continuity.
- **HARD — no timecode without LOOK:** before any comment on a specific timestamp, actually LOOK (full playback or successful frame Read). Desk inference ≠ look.

---

## Unique forward motion (HARD)

- Unique forward motion only.
- No reverse / ping-pong as timeline body.
- No stills / Ken Burns as body — **Ken Burns end card only** if needed.
- Prefer unused banked takes over stretching a bad clip.

---

## Music vocal

- Lead vocal = confident masculine silk.
- Never androgynous / female-leaning default for this cut.
- Once a dial is defined, do not leave hedge words for “flexibility.”

---

## Rejection criteria (kill / don’t ship)

- Farm, barn, nest, Saturday-morning cartoon, candy sitcom lighting
- Open beaks, mammal ears, dance/groove, shredded jacket pre-Reveal
- White/brown eagle wings on Littlebird Reveal
- Photoreal deepfake humans · private real names in public packs
- Summary-only QA / skim-as-done
