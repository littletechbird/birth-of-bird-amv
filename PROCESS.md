# Process — Birth of Bird

Public pipeline for the ~7-minute anthem AMV. Sister lessons from Fairy Tale / Equals / AMV Queen / You / Tesla Flywheel still apply; Birth of Bird adds **lyric-anchor desk**, **unique forward motion only**, and a **Real LOOK before Done** gate.

---

## Pipeline (locked order)

```
Paper (thesis / Story Circle / lyrics / craft laws)
  → Suno custom (lyrics-first; chapters B · I · R · D)
  → Imagine stills (bible-locked mileposts)
  → Seedance / Genjutsu pockets (i2v / kinetic)
  → Post-it lyric-anchor desk (stick lyrics → picture)
  → Unique forward motion only (no reverse / ping-pong; no stills / Ken Burns except end card)
  → G_plan = ceil(1.5 × (N − 1)) unique real-motion gens
  → Chip-away assemble (ffmpeg hard cuts)
  → Real LOOK before Done (full playback QA)
  → Real-ESRGAN animevideov3 → 4K
  → YouTube + X (when public URLs land)
```

Do not skip Paper. Do not lip-sync theater. Do not pad length with reverse, ping-pong, or still zooms.

## 2. Lyrics-first Suno

- Mode: **custom** with **full locked lyrics** (not vibe-only prompts).
- Form: four chapters ≈ **B · I · R · D** (chapter air / silence OK between).
- Vocal bar: confident masculine lead silk — drama weight (Epic of BLUE / 808 lane), not comedy pop.
- Length path: chapter gens → stitch / extend toward the ~7 min anthem master.
- Song clock owns the cut — picture follows the music, not the reverse.

Suno cost lived as **subscription / session** — not an API receipt in this burn sheet.

---

## 3. Imagine stills

- Milepost stills aligned to half-split anchors (classic planning: **N ≈ 22** for a ~4-min skeleton; scale **N** with locked song length for the full anthem).
- Style: cinematic anime drama — Akira / Runaway weight; Neo-Tokyo density; **not** Saturday-morning cartoon.
- Identity language every prompt: bird people (human body + bird head), closed beaks, jackets hide wings until Reveal, Littlebird cyan head + orange bomber, Falcon soft-color jacket, no mammal ears, no tail.
- Zero on-screen text / logos / UI chrome (except the intentional red **BIRD** title card).

---

## 4. Seedance / Genjutsu pockets

| Lane | Job |
|------|-----|
| **Seedance** (hero i2v) | Silk holds, identity, reveal / rupture peaks — start (+ end) frame pins |
| **Genjutsu** | Kinetic action + moving camera (blast, chase, search) — feed real action refs, not silk-hold stills |
| **Imagine Video** | Volume pockets between anchors when appropriate |

Motion notes:

- Mute native i2v audio; the Suno track owns energy.
- Prefer short keepable clips; reject melt / continuity fails early.
- Unused keepers beat new generations when a banked take already works.
- Continuity-first: escalate drift before inventing a new look.

---

## 5. Post-it lyric-anchor desk

Physical / desk discipline (public description):

1. Lock lyric waypoints to timecodes (sticky order = story order).
2. Place picture keepers against lyric anchors — **sudoku**, not slideshow vanity.
3. Half-split planning: pocket length **Δ ≈ D / (N − 1)** between consecutive anchors.
4. Tail-end only after bricks are stuck: fill holes with character-free city establishers (Neo-Tokyo as third character).
5. Pandora closed late: no new character images that break exact continuity — finish with keepers + character-free ests.

---

## 6. Unique forward motion only (HARD)

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Unique real forward motion clips | Reverse / ping-pong to fake length |
| Hard cuts; chip-away local surgery | Freeze pads / still holds as body |
| Ken Burns **only** on end card (if needed) | Ken Burns / still zooms as timeline filler |
| Prefer unused banked takes | Stretch a bad clip |
| Mood-match lyric energy | Lip-sync theater |

**Gen headroom (HARD):** plan **`G_plan = ceil(1.5 × (N − 1))`** unique real-motion gens up front. Purpose: minimize friction from needing to use bad footage. Never assemble short and pad.

---

## 7. Real LOOK before Done (HARD)

- Full desktop playback (or successful frame-level Read) before any “Done,” publish, or timestamp comment.
- Failed grab + talk = fake look. Desk inference ≠ look.
- Same bar for song masters: full listen, not waveform-only.
- Attest real watch / listen in notes — never skim-as-done.

---

When the master picture clears Real LOOK:

- Target: **4K** delivery encode for YouTube
- Do not upscale a cut that failed LOOK — fix motion / continuity first

## Lessons carried in

1. **Short clips** beat heroic long generations.
2. **Fast select** — decide keepers while energy is hot.
3. **Chip-away** — local surgery, not nuclear regen.
4. **Mood-match, not lip-sync.**
5. **No freeze pads** / reverse / ping-pong as body.
6. **No whole-timeline regen** for a local flaw.
7. Paper before pixels; song clock before vanity length.
8. **Real LOOK** — whatever it takes.
9. **G_plan = ceil(1.5 × (N − 1))** unique motion headroom.
10. Story Circle steps map onto every plate: which step does this serve?
