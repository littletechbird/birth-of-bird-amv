## Gender / scale read (HARD)

- Girl vs boy birds must be **clear**.
- Birdneda (eagle): lock the look; do not drift; keep young.
- Falcon: clearer **she** — soft-color jacket, softer hands, kind shy.
- Littlebird: **boy** runt — orange bomber intact, cyan head, smallest.

---

## World (shared)

- Cast = **bird people** (human body + bird head).
- World = **IRL city / Neo-Tokyo** — cars, buildings, neon, weather, density.
- **Never barn. Never nest. Never happy farm.**
- “Coop” in story = urban hangout / structure if needed — not farm barn.
- Coop explosion = **fall into dark**, not true flight yet.
- Red bag = trail / missing motif (prefer still-only when i2v melts FG props).
- Shadow dragon = inner storm dissolving — no gore; nobody dies.

---

## Do

- Cinematic anime drama; dark neon; restrained saturation with cyan/orange character locks
- Closed beaks; eyes carry weight
- Jackets hide wings until Reveal
- Medium-with-air framing so Neo-Tokyo can breathe
- Establishing shots where the city is a third character

## Don’t

- Open beaks / lipsync fight
- Mammal ears
- Dance / groove / mascot bounce
- White or brown eagle wings on Littlebird at Reveal
- Black Falcon jacket
- Littlebird taller/bigger than peers
- Farm / barn / nest / toy-set backgrounds
- On-screen text / logos (except intentional red **BIRD** title)
