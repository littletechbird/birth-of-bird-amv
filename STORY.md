# Birth of Bird — Story

Public thesis + Story Circle map. Story direction sourced from **Dan Harmon’s Story Circle** (Adult Swim lecture): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RG4WcRAgm7Y

---

## Thesis (one-liner)

Littlebird went looking for his ability to fly, in order to fit in. He struck out into the dark, to seek and find. In the process he got distracted to save Falcon from danger. While doing so, he found that he could in fact soar — and always could — into the stars, and just needed the courage found from proving yourself doing something that is in fact dangerous in order to save the innocent, despite the “distraction” from the original goal. Thereby accomplishing the original goal. Returning back to home, but different, having grown. **BIRD.**

Shorter publish line: **We’re all littlebird needing to be hatched.**

## Story Circle

| Step | Bird |
|------|------|
| **1 YOU** | Littlebird / from down here / runt who can’t fly yet |
| **2 NEED** | Ability to fly — to fit in |
| **3 GO** | Strike into the dark (night / pressure / leave comfort) |
| **4 SEARCH** | Seek flight; road of trials (one taste, rupture, missing) |
| **5 FIND** | Falcon in peril — the “distraction” that is the find |
| **6 TAKE** | Save the innocent at real danger; cost / courage; wings prove true (always could) |
| **7 RETURN** | Ocean / friends / equal circle / home |
| **8 CHANGED** | Birth of Bird — grown; not the same runt |

---

## Circle board wording (Harmon)

1. **YOU** — character you can identify with  
2. **NEED** — some kind of need, wish, incompletion  
3. **GO** — across a threshold where the story changes direction  
4. **SEARCH** — road of trials, searching for something  
5. **FIND** — they find it, whether they like it or not  
6. **TAKE** — it kind of kicks their ass  
7. **RETURN** — come back to the world they started in  
8. **CHANGED** — having changed

## Picture consequences (HARD)

- Protect / save Falcon must read as care — not argument, not a twin of an early meet beat.
- Reveal is **courage-earned soar** from the save — not a costume trick.
- “Distraction from original goal” is intentional irony: the save *is* how flight arrives.
- Title / home = RETURN + CHANGED, not a long dead hold.
- POV stays **Littlebird’s**.

### Do not

- Frame Falcon as enemy / conflict opponent.
- Split “learn to fly” and “save Falcon” into unrelated plots.
- Imply flight was gifted by someone else without the courage beat.
- Dark-villain the Eagle (Birdneda) — Eagle remains a friend (soar-over yearning; later search).

### Cast beat (high level)

- Danger Falcon needs saving **from** = Dark Buzz Boys (pressure / keep-the-runt energy) — not Eagle, not Littlebird vs Falcon.
- First Dark-BB fight: Littlebird **loses**. Second: after evil-within, wings out, **saves** Falcon.
- Light Buzz Boys + Eagle + Falcon + Littlebird = end friendship circle.
- Purple / evil-within **after** explosion-fall only.
- Nobody dies. Chooses good. Ocean calms. Friends return.

## Cut QA use

- When ordering stills / waypoints, ask: which Story Circle step does this plate serve?
- One taste / rupture should feel SEARCH → FIND, not early fluff.
- Reveal / choose = TAKE (cost + change), not just pretty wings.
- Title home = CHANGED, not a long slideshow.
